Week 2 Goal

Rough target for this lab: build a Flask web app that takes the circuit parameters, computes the BJT DC bias (VB, VE, VC, VCE, IB, IC, IE) for a common-emitter amplifier with divider, fixed, or emitter bias, and displays the results.
